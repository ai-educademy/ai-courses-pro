## Overview

Prepare concise, honest stories about ambiguity, delivery, failure, influence, conflict, and responsible AI decisions using evidence rather than rehearsed slogans.

This private repo contains Pro subscriber lessons for this programme.

## Topics

- Evidence-led STAR stories
- Ambiguity and prioritisation
- Failure, learning, and recovery
- Conflict and influence
- Responsible AI judgement

## Outcomes

- Build a reusable story bank
- Answer with context, action, and measurable outcome
- Show judgement when technology and people trade-offs collide
- Adapt one experience to different question styles
